Quantum Microscope: L*/Z^d visualized

Blue dots: quantum samples = elements of L*/Z^2. These are what Regev's quantum circuit produces.

Red dots: classical orbit = (2^k mod N, 3^k mod N)/N. This is what classical computation can produce.

Blue arrow (v_α): step in the p-direction. Red arrow (v_β): step in the q-direction.

The quantum lattice is generated by v_α and v_β. Classical sees a 1D path through this 2D lattice. Factoring = knowing which direction is v_α and which is v_β.
